What happens in the water

Cold plunge timeline

10 secondsCold shock. Your breathing quickens and your body switches into alert mode.
10 to 45 secondsNoradrenaline and dopamine start to rise. This is where the buzz comes from.
45 secondsSlow your breathing down. Your heart rate and breathing start to settle.
1 minuteYour body works harder to keep you warm, so your metabolism steps up.
3 minutesCold thermogenesis kicks in: your body starts making its own heat.

Everyone responds differently. Start with short dips, build up slowly, and check with your GP first if you have a heart condition or are pregnant.
