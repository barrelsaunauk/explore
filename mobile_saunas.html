Handmade saunas, bespoke trailers

Crafted by us in Wales

Our mobile barrel saunas are designed and built entirely in Wales by our in-house team. Each sauna is handcrafted from start to finish, then mounted on a bespoke, IVA UK tested trailer that we also manufacture ourselves using Welsh steel. This gives us full control over quality, safety and durability, from the sauna build to the chassis beneath it.
