The simple version

What a grant is

A grant is money from the council or the government that helps your business buy something it needs to grow. You do not pay it back.

Councils give grants because growing local businesses create jobs, bring visitors in and keep money in the area. A gym that adds a sauna and cold plunge gives members a reason to stay and new people a reason to join. That is exactly the kind of growth these funds are set up to back.

The catch is the paperwork. A strong application needs a business plan, a cash flow forecast, prices from more than one maker and a clear case for growth. That is the part we take off your hands.

01

You do not pay it back

A grant is not a loan. Once the work is done and the claim is paid, the money is yours to keep, as long as you follow the grant rules.

02

It covers part of the cost

Most grants pay a share of the price, not all of it. Across Wales that share runs from 50% to 75% depending on the scheme.

03

How the money arrives depends on the scheme

Most Welsh council grants pay you back after the work is done and invoiced. Some release money up front, and some pay in stages. We tell you which way yours works before you commit to anything.

Nothing is ordered before the offer letter

Any money paid towards the sauna before the council says yes in writing, deposits included, can make it ineligible. So the build is only ordered once your offer letter arrives.

£

Timing depends on the scheme

Reimbursement, up front or staged. For a claim back scheme, the council pays once the build is paid in full from your business account. We plan the cash flow with you either way.

2

Prices from more than one maker

Most councils ask for comparable quotes for each item. We help you gather what is needed.

%

VAT is usually not covered

Most grants are worked out on the price before VAT, though rules differ between schemes and we check yours. VAT registered businesses can usually reclaim it in the normal way.
