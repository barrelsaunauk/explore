The simple version

What a grant is

A grant is money from the council or the government that helps your business buy something it needs to grow. You do not pay it back.

Councils give grants because growing local businesses create jobs, bring visitors in and keep money in the area. A gym that adds a sauna and cold plunge gives members a reason to stay and new people a reason to join. That is exactly the kind of growth these funds are set up to back.

The catch is the paperwork. A strong application needs a business plan, a cash flow forecast, prices from more than one maker and a clear case for growth. That is the part we take off your hands.

A Welsh makerEvery build carries our nameplate. Many Welsh grants favour local suppliers, and we build every sauna ourselves in Caerphilly.

01

You do not pay it back

A grant is not a loan. Once the work is done and the claim is paid, the money is yours to keep, as long as you follow the grant rules.

02

It covers part of the cost

Most grants pay a share of the price, not all of it. In our example the council pays 75% and your business puts in the rest.

03

It is paid back to you after

You pay for the build first, then claim the grant with your invoices. We plan the timing with you so nothing catches you out.

How the sums work

The Business Growth Grant from Rhondda Cynon Taf council is open now, on a rolling basis. It pays up to 75% of eligible costs, from £2,000 to £15,000, for businesses trading from commercial premises in the area.

!

Nothing is ordered before the offer letter

Any money paid towards the sauna before the council says yes in writing, deposits included, can make it ineligible. So the build is only ordered once your offer letter arrives.

£

You pay first, then claim it back

The council pays the grant once the build is paid for in full by bank transfer from your business account.

2

Prices from two makers

The council asks for comparable prices for each item. We help you gather what is needed.

%

VAT is not covered

The grant is worked out on the price before VAT. VAT registered businesses can usually reclaim it in the normal way.
